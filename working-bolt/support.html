Common questions

How do I sign in?

Open the app and sign in with your own Lightning Bolt username and password on the Lightning Bolt page that appears. The app never sees or stores your password.

It says my session expired / a screen looks empty.

Your Lightning Bolt session timed out. Sign out and back in from the More tab, then pull to refresh. Your cached shifts stay available offline in the meantime.

I'm not getting shift alerts.

Make sure notifications are enabled for Working-Bolt in iOS Settings → Notifications. Alerts are sent only for newly opened shifts relevant to the group.

How is my data handled?

Your schedule is cached on your device; only open-shift postings, your employee ID, and your notification token are sent to our private backend to power alerts and the shared pool. See the Privacy Policy for details. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Just want to look around first?

On the sign-in screen, tap “Explore with sample data” to try the whole app with example shifts — no login needed.
