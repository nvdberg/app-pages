Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

vdB Courses (“the app”) is a private reader for a personal library of interactive study material. It is a family and small-group tool, not a service. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version. The app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no backend. Every course is bundled inside the app and read from the device itself. What you have read stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, asks for no personal information, and sends nothing about you to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server behind it to send anything to.

Data stored on your device

The app records which lessons you have completed, which questions you answered incorrectly so it can offer them again, and any bookmarks. This is written to the app’s own storage on your device. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests of its own and works fully offline. The course documents themselves reference two web fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) hosted by Google Fonts. When the device is online, the system may therefore request those font files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which will see the request as any website visit would. No information about you or your reading is included in that request, and when offline the app substitutes the system typeface and carries on.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No accounts, passwords, email addresses, or contact information.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data — there is none to share.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health data access.

Profiles on a shared device

The app can be set to a student profile, which limits it to the courses intended for that reader. This is a display setting stored on the device. It is a convenience, not a security boundary, and it involves no accounts and no personal data.

Children

The app is used by students as well as adults. Because it collects no data whatsoever, nothing is gathered from any user regardless of age.

Medical and technical content

Some courses cover clinical and technical subjects. They are educational material only — not medical advice, not a protocol, and not a substitute for a manufacturer’s instructions or professional judgement.
